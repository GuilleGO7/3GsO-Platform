# User Journey

This document owns **visitor profiles and journey flow**.

The destinations these journeys move through are defined in `02_Experience_Architecture.md`. The audience definition is owned by `00_Project_Vision.md`.

---

## Journey Goal

Every visitor should quickly understand who Guillermo is, find evidence of his capabilities, and decide how deeply they want to explore.

The experience adapts to intent without creating separate or disconnected experiences. It never forces a predefined path.

**Curiosity → Understanding → Evidence → Potential → Connection**

---

## Visitor Profiles

### Recruiter / Hiring Manager

Evaluating Guillermo as a candidate. Interested in experience, capability, relevant projects, career direction, evidence of ownership, and how he approaches problems.

Must be able to form a strong impression quickly, with deeper evidence available on demand.

### Senior Professional / Decision-Maker

Evaluating potential beyond current experience. Interested in strategic thinking, business understanding, problem-solving, ability to learn, and direction of development.

Should leave understanding where Guillermo could grow next, not only where he has been.

### Entrepreneur / Founder / Peer

Arrives through a project, an idea or a shared link. Interested in products, decisions, trade-offs, ways of working and possible collaboration.

Should be drawn into the reasoning behind the work.

### General Visitor

No specific professional objective. Should find enough context to create interest without being overwhelmed.

---

## Primary Journey

**Arrival → Orientation → Discovery → Depth → Connection**

**Arrival** — the visitor encounters a clear expression of identity and direction on `/`. The first interaction creates curiosity and a reason to continue.

**Orientation** — a concise understanding of who Guillermo is, what he does, what interests him and where he is heading.

**Discovery** — selected work, milestones and ideas that provide evidence of capability. Curated by relevance, not chronology or completeness.

**Depth** — `/work/[slug]`, `/journey` and `/thinking/[slug]` reveal reasoning, decisions and outcomes rather than simply adding information.

**Connection** — once interest exists, `/connect` makes reaching out effortless. Contact is a natural conclusion, never a forced conversion.

---

## Journeys by Profile

Each journey uses the same four destinations. Only the entry point and the order change.

### Recruiter / Hiring Manager

1. **Identify** — `/` establishes professional identity, direction and ambition.
2. **Validate** — `/work` and `/journey` provide concrete evidence.
3. **Differentiate** — self-initiated projects, product thinking, ownership and continuous learning distinguish him from a conventional candidate.
4. **Explore** — a full-depth project supplies stronger evidence where required.
5. **Connect** — `/connect` is reachable from anywhere, at any point.

The journey minimizes friction between interest and conversation.

### Senior Professional / Decision-Maker

1. **Context** — current position and trajectory.
2. **Perspective** — `/thinking` and the reasoning inside projects show how he approaches problems.
3. **Evidence** — selected work demonstrates turning ideas into outcomes.
4. **Potential** — the closing section of `/journey` makes clear that current experience is a stage in a longer trajectory.
5. **Connection** — a direct, natural path to a conversation.

### Entrepreneur / Founder / Peer

1. **Discover** — a project, idea or perspective creates interest.
2. **Explore** — contextual links surface related work and thinking.
3. **Understand** — reasoning, decisions and trade-offs are visible.
4. **Connect** — collaboration is possible without the experience feeling transactional.

## Project-Driven Journey

Many visitors arrive directly at `/work/[slug]` from LinkedIn, search or a shared link. **A project page must work with no prior context.**

A visitor landing there must be able to understand what the project is, why it exists, what problem it addresses, Guillermo's role, what decisions were made and what was learned.

It must then offer contextual paths onward: other projects, the journey behind it, related thinking, and Connect.

This journey is why per-page metadata and social preview images are foundational rather than an optimization step (`10_Implementation_Roadmap.md`, Phases 1 and 3).

---

## Returning Visitor Journey

Returning visitors should find a platform that is visibly alive: new projects, deeper work, new ideas, new milestones, changes in direction.

The platform communicates continuous development while preserving the core identity of 3G'sO.

---

## Journey Principles

**Follow intent, not a script** — visitors follow their own interests rather than a fixed sequence.

**Reveal gradually** — enough to create context, then depth on demand.

**Evidence over claims** — capability shown through work, decisions and outcomes.

**Context before detail** — why something matters comes before how it was done.

**No dead ends** — every page, including 404, offers a meaningful next step.

**Respect attention** — communicate value quickly; avoid unnecessary interaction and decorative complexity.

**Create curiosity** — each stage encourages discovery of the next.

**Earn the connection** — establish context and trust before inviting contact.

**Keep evolving** — the journey stays relevant as career, projects and ambitions develop.

---

## Journey Success

A successful journey is one in which the visitor:

1. Understands who Guillermo is.
2. Understands what he is capable of.
3. Finds evidence supporting that impression.
4. Understands where he is heading.
5. Can explore further if interested.
6. Can connect effortlessly when appropriate.

The objective is not to maximize clicks or time on the platform. It is to create a clear, memorable and credible understanding of Guillermo and his potential.
