# Content Strategy

This document owns **what is written and how it is structured editorially**.

Where that content lives is owned by `02_Experience_Architecture.md`. How it is modelled technically is owned by `08_Project_Structure.md`.

---

## Content Purpose

Content is the platform. It should reveal how Guillermo thinks, what he values, what he can build and where he intends to grow.

Every piece contributes to one narrative:

**Who Guillermo is → What he does → How he thinks → What he is building → Where he is going**

Content stays authentic to current experience while communicating ambition and direction.

---

## Content Pillars

Three pillars, matching the three destinations that hold content.

### Work

What Guillermo has built and learned through real projects and professional experience.

### Thinking

How he approaches problems, opportunities, technology, business, strategy, products and personal development.

### Journey

Who he is, how his education, experiences and decisions shaped him — and where he is heading next.

**Direction and future ambition are a thread running through all three, not a fourth pillar.** They surface as the closing movement of Journey and as a recurring lens in Thinking.

---

## Personal Narrative

The personal narrative communicates Guillermo as a person, not only as a professional profile: curiosity, ambition, discipline, continuous growth, resilience, trust in the process, and a long-term perspective.

It opens `/journey` and, in condensed form, anchors the homepage. Both draw from a single source so they cannot drift apart.

It provides context and personality without becoming an unrelated personal diary.

---

## Professional Narrative

The professional narrative describes an evolving profile at the intersection of technology, business, strategy and product thinking.

Engineering is the foundation, not the boundary. The content should show growing interest in business, strategy, product, financial environments, leadership and decision-making — communicating direction without claiming expertise that has not been earned.

Potential is demonstrated through curiosity, initiative, projects, learning and the quality of thinking.

## Work Content

Work content is meaningful evidence, not exhaustive documentation.

### The Project Narrative

Every project follows one canonical structure. This is the single authoritative version; no alternative structure exists.

1. **Context** — the situation, the environment and the constraints that shaped it.
2. **Problem** — what actually needed solving, and why it mattered.
3. **Role** — what Guillermo personally owned.
4. **Approach** — how the problem was attacked, including the challenges encountered.
5. **Key Decisions** — the choices made, the alternatives considered and the reasoning. *This section carries the most weight.*
6. **Outcome** — what resulted, honestly stated.
7. **Learnings** — what was learned, what would be done differently, and where the work could go next.

### Two Depths, One Structure

**Brief** — sections 1–5, concise. Enough for a reader to understand the work and judge its substance.

**Full** — all seven sections, written in depth. This is what "case study" means at 3G'sO: **a project written at full depth, not a separate kind of page.** There is no separate case-study format, route or template.

Full depth is reserved for work where deeper exploration provides real value. At least one project must be at full depth in v1.

Technical implementation appears where it adds meaningful context. Technology supports the story; it never becomes the story.

### Project Selection

Not every project deserves equal visibility. Prioritize by impact, relevance, complexity, ownership, learning and contribution to the overall narrative.

A small number of strong projects is always preferable to a large collection of mediocre ones.

## Thinking Content

Thinking demonstrates reasoning and perspective. Possible subjects include problem-solving, technology and its impact on business, strategy, product thinking, professional development, leadership, decision-making, building projects, lessons from failure, learning new disciplines, and maintaining perspective through difficult periods.

The objective is not authority on every topic. It is thoughtful curiosity and the ability to develop and communicate an idea.

Personal reflections may explore how to approach challenges, stay calm under uncertainty and recognize opportunities — grounded in real experience, never motivational content without substance.

### Publication Standard

Thinking is published only when there is something genuinely worth saying.

**A minimum of two strong pieces is required before the section appears publicly.** An empty or thin Thinking section damages the platform more than its absence does. This standard is a content decision, not a technical constraint — see `09_Feature_Specification.md`.

## Journey Content

Journey content provides the context for Guillermo's evolution: education, professional experience, major projects, milestones, important decisions, new interests, lessons and changes in direction.

Every entry is the same kind of thing — a moment in an evolution — distinguished by its type rather than separated into different sections.

It must not reproduce a chronological résumé. Chronology may provide structure, but meaning takes priority over dates:

**Experience → Learning → Evolution → Direction**

rather than:

**Date → Position → Description**

Journey entries reference the projects they produced, so the two areas connect through content rather than through hardcoded links.

---

## Content Curation

Content is curated, not accumulated. Before publishing, it should answer yes to at least one of:

- Does this demonstrate meaningful capability?
- Does this reveal how Guillermo thinks?
- Does this contribute to his professional narrative?
- Does this demonstrate meaningful growth?
- Does this provide useful perspective?
- Does this represent something worth remembering?

If not, it should not be published.

Fewer strong pieces beat frequent low-value updates.

---

## Content Evolution

3G'sO evolves with Guillermo's career. As experience grows, the balance between technical, strategic, business, product and leadership content may shift.

Older content does not stay prominent simply because it came first.

**Emerging → Relevant → Established → Historical**

Content that becomes less representative remains accessible with less prominence. Significant milestones are preserved as part of the journey.

New areas of development — new industries, leadership, entrepreneurial projects, products, research — must fit the three existing pillars rather than requiring a new content architecture.

## Writing Principles

**Clarity** — write directly; avoid jargon when simpler language communicates the same idea.

**Substance** — every statement communicates something. Avoid generic professional claims that evidence cannot support.

**Confidence without exaggeration** — ambition and confidence, never overstated experience.

**Authenticity** — sound like a real person, not a corporate brand.

**Thoughtfulness** — favour nuance and reasoning over absolute statements.

**Brevity** — the minimum text that communicates the idea well. Length is earned by the subject, never used to appear sophisticated.

**Consistency** — one voice across projects, journey, thinking and personal content, aligned with `01_Brand_Identity.md`.

---

## Content Rules

- Never fabricate or exaggerate experience, achievements or expertise.
- Never publish content solely to fill space.
- Never turn a project into a list of technologies.
- Prioritize decisions and outcomes over implementation details.
- Show evidence whenever making a meaningful professional claim.
- Preserve authenticity when discussing personal experience.
- Keep ambition visible without turning the platform into self-promotion.
- Prefer thoughtful perspective over generic motivational statements.
- Review content as the career develops.
- Preserve meaningful milestones even when no longer current.
- Every piece of content should strengthen the overall 3G'sO narrative.
