# Project Vision

## Overview

3G'sO is the digital identity of Guillermo Guzmán González Ortiz.

It is a long-term professional platform: a place to show how he thinks, decides and builds, through real projects, professional experience and continuous learning.

It is not a portfolio, and it is not a static snapshot of a career. It is intended to evolve for years.

The identity, values and tone that govern everything else are defined in `01_Brand_Identity.md`.

---

## Mission

To demonstrate not only technical capability, but the way Guillermo approaches problems, makes decisions and grows as a professional — creating a digital space that reflects both his current abilities and his long-term direction.

---

## Positioning

Guillermo's engineering background is the foundation of the platform, but it is not the boundary of it.

3G'sO presents an evolving profile at the intersection of technology, product, business and strategy. It communicates direction without claiming expertise that has not yet been earned.

Potential is demonstrated through curiosity, initiative, real work and the quality of thinking — never through inflated titles or claims.

---

## Objectives

- Build a memorable personal identity that reflects who Guillermo is as a professional.
- Demonstrate capability through real projects, decisions and outcomes rather than technology lists.
- Show the combination of engineering, product thinking and strategic interest.
- Complement Guillermo's CV and LinkedIn profile rather than duplicate them.
- Leave a lasting impression on the people who evaluate professional potential.
- Evolve continuously alongside his career.

---

## Audience

This document is the single owner of the audience definition. Visitor profiles and their journeys are defined in `03_User_Journey.md`.

### Primary

Professionals, recruiters, hiring managers and decision-makers from ambitious technology, consulting, strategy and financial organizations.

They want to understand not only what Guillermo has done, but how he thinks, what he can build and where he could grow next.

### Secondary

Entrepreneurs, founders, engineers, designers and other professionals who discover 3G'sO through a project, an idea or a shared link.

---

## Success Criteria

3G'sO is successful if it:

- Communicates Guillermo's professional identity within the first minute.
- Gives visitors a reason to explore beyond the homepage.
- Demonstrates depth through real work rather than lists of technologies.
- Reinforces credibility and growth potential.
- Complements the CV and LinkedIn profile.
- Evolves as his career, projects and interests develop.

These criteria are qualitative by design. No analytics are collected in v1 (see `07_Technology_Architecture.md`), so v1 success is assessed through the launch quality gates in `10_Implementation_Roadmap.md` and through real professional feedback rather than measured engagement.

---

## Naming

The platform is **3G'sO**. The repository and project identity is **`3GsO-Platform`**.

The word "portfolio" is not used as project terminology.
